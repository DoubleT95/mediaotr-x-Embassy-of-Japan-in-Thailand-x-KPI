02 · Define · Shared goals
DiscoverDefineDesignDevelopDeliver

From a one-day celebration
to a network that lasts until the 150th anniversary

Beyond 140 is not designed to end on the day. It is the start of a network of young Thai and Japanese leaders who meet, think together and pass the relationship on — turning remembering the past into building the future.

1
From “dots”one-off events
→
To “lines”A network that meets every year
2
From “audience”one-way listening
→
To “co-creators”All four groups think, propose and act together
3
From “people”a few key individuals
→
To “systems”Common ground that keeps working when people move on
What we will build together
01A 4-group leaders’ platformGet to know each other and share strengths
02“140+ Commitments”Concrete actions each group will take forward
03The Beyond 140 NetworkCommon ground that meets yearly, generation to generation
2027 → 2037Lay the foundation today for a
mature network by the 150th
